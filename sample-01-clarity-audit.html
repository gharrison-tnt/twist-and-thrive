Finding 01 — Follow-Up Gap

Inquiry emails are dying in your inbox

An audit of your intake flow shows that new inquiries submitted via your website form are landing in a shared Gmail inbox with no automation, no auto-reply, and no SLA. During your two busiest months (January and September), average response time exceeded 72 hours. Industry data for wellness studios shows conversion drops by 40% after 24 hours of silence.

You're not losing these leads because your pricing is wrong or your services aren't compelling. You're losing them to a faster competitor who responded first.

The fix: A 3-step automated intake sequence — immediate acknowledgment, qualification question at hour 4, and a calendar link at hour 24 if no reply. Estimated setup time: 2 hours. Estimated annual recovery: $9,600–$14,400.

Finding 02 — Onboarding Gap

New clients are leaving before they understand your value

Clients who book a first session and receive no structured follow-up average 1.4 return visits. Clients who received even an informal "here's what to expect" email averaged 4.3 return visits. The difference isn't your service quality — it's that clients who feel oriented invest more.

Currently there is no onboarding sequence in place. New clients receive a booking confirmation and nothing else until the day before their appointment.

The fix: A 3-email onboarding flow (pre-visit prep, post-visit check-in, 30-day milestone). Reactivates the relationship at each touchpoint. Estimated annual recovery: $11,200–$16,800 from improved retention alone.

Finding 03 — Lapsed Client Re-Engagement

Your warmest leads have gone completely cold

You have 214 clients in your booking system who haven't made an appointment in over 90 days. These clients have already trusted you with their money and time — they're your easiest re-conversion. There is no system to reach them, no trigger that flags inactivity, and no offer designed to win them back.

A conservative 12% re-engagement rate on dormant clients at your average session price ($95) represents immediate recoverable revenue with a single campaign.

The fix: A 2-touch re-engagement sequence triggered at 90 days of inactivity, paired with a "we miss you" offer (complimentary add-on, not a discount). Estimated one-time recovery: $7,600+. Estimated recurring annual value: $18,000+ if systematized.
