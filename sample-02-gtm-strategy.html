GTM Strategy Brief

secret in town.

A focused market entry and growth plan for a functional nutrition practice expanding into corporate wellness. Includes positioning, channel strategy, and a 90-day activation roadmap.

The Problem We're Solving

Great service, wrong audience, unclear message

Roots & Rise has strong retention among existing clients and excellent outcomes data. The gap is upstream: the current website speaks to "anyone interested in wellness" and the referral pipeline is entirely word-of-mouth from a small network. New revenue will require reaching a defined segment with a specific message — not broader visibility.

The corporate wellness channel represents a $2.4M addressable market within a 25-mile radius, with budgets already allocated at target companies. This plan focuses there first.

Who we're going after — and why

Primary ICP — Corporate
HR Directors & People Ops leads at Boulder/Denver tech companies
50–300 employees · $1M+ wellness budget
Pain: employee burnout, benefit underutilization

Secondary ICP — Direct Consumer
Women 34–52, dual-income households
Health-motivated, time-constrained

Channels: Instagram, Google, referral
Deal size: $180–$420 per package

Positioning

The one thing you need to own in the market

Current positioning: "Personalized nutrition for a healthier you" — generic, forgettable, indistinguishable from 40 competitors in the metro area.

Recommended positioning: "The nutrition partner for high-performing teams and the people running them." This owns the intersection of professional performance and personal health — a white space no local competitor holds.

This positioning works for both ICPs: HR Directors buying for their teams, and individual clients who self-identify as high performers. One message, two doors.

90-Day Activation Roadmap

The first moves, in order

  • 1
    Week 1–2: Rewrite website hero and services page with new positioning. Update LinkedIn profile. Create one-page corporate wellness overview PDF.
  • 2
    Week 3–4: Build LinkedIn outreach list of 60 HR targets. Begin 10 personalized outreach messages per week. Set up simple CRM (HubSpot free tier) to track pipeline.
  • 3
    Month 2: Launch content calendar. Begin referral partner conversations. Run a one-time "lunch & learn" event for one target company at no cost to generate case study material.
  • 4
    Month 3: Review pipeline data. Double down on highest-converting channel. Formalize referral partner agreement with top 2 partners.

Revenue target, 90 days: 2 corporate contracts at $12,000 average = $24,000 new ARR. 8 new individual clients at $280 average package = $2,240. Total: $26,240 in new revenue from a standing start.
