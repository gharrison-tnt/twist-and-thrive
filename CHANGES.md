# Site update, October 16, 2026 (second pass)

## The new structure: five levels that build

Each level includes everything before it.

| Level | Starting price | Built like |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Intake | $2,500 | K5 (four forms, one queue), Nurture Sync, the summit's two-path application |
| 2. Dashboard | $4,500 | Reclaim & Renew, Nurture Sync, K5's home screen |
| 3. Checks and balances | $7,000 | K5 matching and document scanning, summit approvals and grading |
| 4. Automate | $9,500 | Summit editable emails, deadline reminders, status links |
| 5. Full system | $14,000 | Summit reporting and settings, plus the outside-reviewer portal (in progress) |

Other pieces of the menu:

- **Free ways in:** the Time Leak Quiz and the Discovery Call
- **Clarity Audit:** $450, credited toward any level started within 30 days
- **Add-ons, at any level:**
  - Payments, from $750, optional because many owners prefer to handle payments themselves
  - Website, from $2,000
  - Brand basics, from $1,000
  - Pricing and offers, from $1,200
- **Outreach and launch planning:** on request
- **After launch:**
  - Hosting and care, from $150 a month. This is new, added after the battlecard research showed that $750 a month loses every comparison against a $49 subscription.
  - Monthly Access, from $750 a month, for ongoing builds

A "How real projects stack up" grid shows K5 at levels 1 to 3, Nurture Sync at levels 1 and 2, and the summit at all five (level 5 marked as in progress).
