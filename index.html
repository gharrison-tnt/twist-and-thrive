Case study — Memory Care Project

Built from the inside
of both worlds.

My father has dementia. I've also spent 15+ years building the operational systems that earn trust at scale inside enterprise organizations. Memory Care Project is what happens when those two experiences meet — a practice vertical I built from scratch to close the gap between the care memory care communities deliver and the experience families and staff actually have.

View the live site →
What was built Brand strategy · 5-step methodology · Free assessment tool · Full website · Service packaging · Go-to-market positioning
The problem it solves Poor family communication drives more move-outs than poor care. Communities needed a structured way to close the gap between excellent care delivery and the experience families and staff actually have.
Status Live. Actively forming engagements with memory care and assisted living communities across the region.
The methodology
A 5-step framework built from two sides of the same problem.
Communication, Trust, Education, Engagement, Responsiveness — five pillars designed to close the family experience gap. Built from 15+ years of operational leadership and the lived reality of being a family caregiver.
The positioning
Named the problem nobody had named.
Most communities talk about care quality. Memory Care Project reframes it: poor family communication drives more move-outs than poor care. That insight became the core positioning — and the organizing principle for every service and every engagement.
The build
A full practice vertical, not just a landing page.
Four service offerings. A free structured assessment. A Calendly-integrated discovery flow. A website that earns trust before the first conversation. Strategy, systems, and brand moving together from the start.
