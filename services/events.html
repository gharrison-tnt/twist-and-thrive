Events

Event sites and signups that look like your event, not a template

Running your event on Wix or Eventbrite? If the site feels generic and you're sorting signups by hand, there's a better fit: a branded site, an application or registration built for your event, and a checkout that doesn't take a cut of every ticket.

What I build for events

  • An event website: overview, agenda, speakers and partners, sponsors, travel, and FAQ pages, written in your voice
  • An application or registration form with a different path for each kind of attendee
  • A review screen to approve, decline, or grade each application
  • A branded checkout on Stripe that confirms people automatically
  • Purchase deadlines with reminders and an expired-seat notice
  • A status page and one-click links, so people check where they stand without emailing you
  • Emails you can edit yourself: received, approved, not selected, payment confirmed, add to calendar
  • Reporting on who has applied and who has paid

Good fits

  • Invite-only summits and conferences where you review who attends
  • Retreats and workshops with limited spots
  • Programs and cohorts that take applications
  • Events with more than one kind of attendee, like investors and founders, or sponsors and guests

Running a simple, open event? A ticketing platform may be all you need, and I'll tell you so.

Questions

Do you take a cut of ticket sales?

No. Tickets are sold through a Stripe account in your name. Stripe charges its standard processing fee, and I charge nothing per ticket.

Can I keep using Eventbrite or Wix?

Yes. Some owners keep their ticketing and only add a branded application and review screen. We'll decide together what's worth replacing.

Can I reuse it next year?

Yes. The system stays yours, so next year's event starts from this year's site, forms, and emails instead of from scratch.

Do I need my own Stripe account?

Yes, in your name, so the money goes straight to you. I'll help you set it up if you don't have one.

Planning an event?

Twenty minutes, free. Tell me about the event and how people sign up today. I'll tell you honestly what's worth building.
