A website that sounds like you and makes booking easy

Most of your clients hear about you from someone they trust. Then they look you up. Your website's job is to make them feel like they already know you, and to make booking easy.

What I build

  • Websites up to 5 pages, written in your voice
  • Booking and intake built into the site
  • Link-in-bio pages for Instagram
  • Brand basics: logo, colors, type
  • Event and program pages
  • Setup so Google and AI search tools can find and describe you

This is for you if

  • You don't have a website, or you're embarrassed by the one you have
  • People find you on Instagram but can't figure out how to book
  • Your site was written by someone who didn't know your work

Questions

Do you write the words too?

Yes. That's most of the work. I interview you and write it in your voice, then you edit until it sounds right.

What platform do you build on?

Whatever fits. Some clients need a site they can edit themselves. Others want something custom and fast. We decide together before I start.

Will people be able to find me?

I set up every site with the basics search engines and AI tools look for: clear page titles, descriptions, and structured information about your business.
