Charge what your work is worth, without losing the clients you have

You give a lot of yourself to your work. Your prices should let you keep doing it. Most owners set their prices once and then live with them. I look at what you offer, what businesses around you charge, and what your clients actually value. Then we build packages that are easy to say yes to.

What I build

  • A look at what 5 to 8 businesses near you charge
  • A package and price structure that fits your capacity
  • A client-facing price sheet or service menu
  • Talking points for telling current clients about new rates
  • Ideas for new offers using what you already do

This is for you if

  • You haven't raised prices in a while and you're nervous to
  • Clients ask for discounts and you say yes
  • You're booked out but not making what you should
  • Your services are hard to explain on one page
