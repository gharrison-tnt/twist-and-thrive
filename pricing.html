What to expect

Pricing questions

How much does it cost to work with Twist & Thrive Systems?

Booking with reminders is $200. A Clarity Audit is $450. The three main offers start at $1,200, $2,000, and $2,200. Custom portals and apps start at $6,500. Monthly Access is $750 a month. Every price is set before work starts.

How do I get started?

Book a free 20-minute Discovery Call, or take the 3-minute Time Leak Quiz first if you'd like to see where your time goes. Either way, you get a clear price before you say yes to anything.

What's the difference between the quiz and the Clarity Audit?

The Time Leak Quiz is a free 3-minute self-check. It estimates where your week goes from your own answers. The Clarity Audit is me looking at your actual business and writing up what to build first, in order.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. You pay for what gets built, not for my time. We agree on the deliverable and the price up front.

How long does it take?

It depends on the build and on my schedule when we start. Your written scope includes your delivery dates, before you pay anything.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments. Custom builds are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.
