How paying works

You get a written scope and price before anything starts. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments. Card or bank transfer.

Working with someone you know?

If we know each other, ask. I'll show you the full price first, then what I can do. I'd rather split payments or phase the work than cut corners on what you get.

Pricing questions

How much does it cost to work with Twist & Thrive Systems?

Small builds start at $200. A Clarity Audit is $350, credited toward anything you start within 30 days. Packages start at $900. Custom portals and apps start at $4,500. Monthly Access starts at $750 a month. Every price is set before work starts.

Is anything free?

The 20-minute Kickoff Call. That's it. Everything else is paid, and you'll know the price before you say yes.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. You pay for what gets built, not for my time. We agree on the deliverable and the price up front.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments. Custom builds are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.

What's in the Clarity Audit?

A written look at your business: what's working, what's stuck, and what I'd build first, in order. We walk through it together. If you start a build or package within 30 days, the $350 comes off the price.
