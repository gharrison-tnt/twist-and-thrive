What to expect

Timing

Timelines depend on the build and on my schedule when we start. Your written scope includes your delivery dates, counted from when I have what I need from you.

You own it

Each client gets their own accounts. Your data stays exportable, and you can have a full copy of the code whenever you want. Where your systems and data live.

Running costs

Hosting is covered by Hosting and care. Tools you choose to add, like Stripe, have their own fees, paid to them directly. I tell you what anything new will cost before you sign up.

Health information

I don't build systems that store people's medical or health records. If your intake needs them, I'll connect you to a HIPAA-compliant tool instead.

Why work with me

Before Twist & Thrive, I spent 15 years running customer experience, onboarding, and operations at software companies like Conga and Movable Ink, setting up systems for large enterprise clients. Now I build the same kind of thing, sized for a small business. When you work with me, you work with me.

How paying works

You get a written scope and price before anything starts. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments. Card or bank transfer.

Pricing questions

How much does it cost to work with Twist & Thrive Systems?

Levels start at $2,500 for Intake and go up to $14,000 and more for a full system. Add-ons start at $750. A Clarity Audit is $450, credited when you start a level. Hosting and care starts at $150 a month. Every price is set before work starts.

Which level do I need?

Most owners start at Level 1 or 2. Take the 3-minute Time Leak Quiz to see where your time goes, or book a free call and I'll tell you which level fits.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. That's how the levels are designed. Each one builds on the last, so moving up adds to what you have instead of replacing it.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. You pay for what gets built, not for my time. We agree on the deliverable and the price up front.

How long does it take?

It depends on the build and on my schedule when we start. Your written scope includes your delivery dates, before you pay anything.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments.
