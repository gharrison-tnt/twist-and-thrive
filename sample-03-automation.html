Recommended Stack

Tools to implement — in priority order

  • 1
    Jane App (or upgrade current EHR) — digital intake forms, automated reminders, online booking. Replaces 4+ hours of manual admin weekly. If already on Jane, 3 automations are inactive and need to be turned on. High impact · Low effort
  • 2
    Zapier + Gmail — automated billing follow-up sequence triggered 3, 7, and 14 days after invoice sent. No new tool required — connects what you already have. Setup: 45 minutes. High impact · Low effort
  • 3
    Birdeye or NiceJob — automated review request sent via SMS 24 hours post-visit. Shown to increase Google review volume by 3–5x in service businesses. Current Google rating: 4.1. Target: 4.6+ within 90 days. Medium impact · Low effort
  • 4
    Buffer + Claude (AI draft) — batch-create 3 weeks of social content in one 90-minute session using AI-assisted drafting. Schedule via Buffer. Eliminates daily context-switching. Medium impact · Low effort
  • 5
    Tali or Nuance DAX — AI-assisted clinical note dictation. Reduces documentation time by ~40%. Medium implementation lift — requires provider training and EHR integration check. High impact · Medium effort
