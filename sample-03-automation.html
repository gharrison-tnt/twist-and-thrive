Operations audit and custom software build for a property cleanout and estate services company. The engagement identified where the business was leaking time and revenue — then delivered a working platform to close the gap.

What We Found

Paper-based operations in a growth-stage business.

Clearpath was running a multi-job, multi-crew operation on text messages, spreadsheets, and memory. There was no shared system for tracking what was in a property, what was being removed, what had salvage value, or what had been completed. Every job was a fresh coordination problem.

The Clarity Audit identified three core friction points: client communication was ad hoc, job status existed only in the owner's head, and salvage revenue — a meaningful secondary income stream — was being tracked informally and likely undercounted.

Core finding

"The business had outgrown the tools they were using to run it. They needed a system, not a spreadsheet."

Workflow Audit

Every manual process, mapped and assessed.

What We Built

A live operations platform. Not a recommendation.

Most automation engagements end with a tool stack recommendation and an implementation to-do list. This one ended with a working product. Reclaim & Renew is a custom web application built during the engagement — live and in active use by the client.

Feature

Job Dashboard

All active jobs in one view. Crew and owner see the same real-time status. No more "what's happening on Oak Street" texts.

Feature

Item Board

Three-bucket status system per item: Remove, Keep, Review. Every item gets a photo and a comment thread. Decisions are documented in place.

Feature

Salvage Revenue Tracker

Items flagged for sale are tracked through to close-out. Salvage revenue is captured per job and surfaced in the dashboard — no more estimating.

Feature

Job Close-Out Modal

Structured end-of-job documentation with final revenue totals, completion notes, and a record the owner can share with clients or reference for follow-on work.

How We Got There

Audit first. Build second.

The platform wasn't the starting point — the audit was. We identified what needed to exist before writing a line of code.

1
Clarity Audit

One-hour structured conversation mapping every workflow, tool, and pain point in the current operation. Produced a written findings document before any solution work began.

2
Product Requirements Document

Translated audit findings into a scoped PRD — what to build, what to defer, and why. Gave the client a clear artifact to react to before development started.

3
Iterative Build

Built the platform in phases with the client in the room — reviewing, giving feedback, and shaping features in real time. No handoff surprise at the end.

4
Live Deployment

The application was deployed to production at the end of the engagement. The client left with a working tool, not a plan to build one.
