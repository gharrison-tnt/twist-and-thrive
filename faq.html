Questions

Straight answers about pricing, process, and what it's like to work together.

How much does it cost to work with Twist & Thrive Systems?

Small builds start at $200. A Clarity Audit is $450 on its own, and included when you go on to a package or custom build within 30 days. Packages start at $1,200. Custom portals and apps start at $6,500. Monthly Access starts at $750 a month. Every price is set before work starts.

How do I get started?

Book a free 20-minute Discovery Call. We talk through what you're working on and what's slowing it down. From there you get a clear price before you say yes to anything. If you go on to a package or custom build, the Clarity Audit is included.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. You pay for what gets built, not for my time. We agree on the deliverable and the price up front.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small, independent businesses. Wellness practitioners, doulas, trainers, breeders, solo nonprofits, local service businesses. Usually the owner is doing most of the work themselves.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I build it, show you how it works, and stay around long enough to make sure you're comfortable using it.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Where I can, I build inside your own accounts. Custom apps get their own setup so you can take them with you if we ever stop working together.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Anything over $1,000 can be split into two or three payments. Custom builds are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the build. Quick builds are small by design. You get a delivery date before you pay.

Do you use AI?

Yes, the way a carpenter uses a power saw. It helps me build faster. It doesn't decide what to build. The thinking and the judgment are mine.

Where are you located?

Boulder County, Colorado. I meet in person around Boulder, Lafayette, Louisville, and Denver, and work remotely with businesses anywhere in the US.

What happens on the Discovery Call?

Twenty minutes. You tell me what's pulling you away from your clients. I ask a few questions and tell you honestly whether I can help, what I'd start with, and roughly what it would cost. No pitch.

What's in the Clarity Audit?

A written look at your business: what's working, what's stuck, and what I'd build first, in order. We walk through it together. On its own it's $450. If you go on to a package or custom build within 30 days, it's included.

Question not here?

Ask on the call, or email me at consultwithatwist@gmail.com.
