Phase 1 — Onboarding Sequence

The first 30 days set everything

Most clients who don't return aren't unhappy — they're just not thinking about you. The onboarding sequence keeps you present during the window where habits form and second visits are decided.

TouchpointTimingChannelContent
Welcome Immediately after booking Email What to expect at your first visit, parking, what to wear, what to bring
Pre-visit prep 24 hours before SMS "We're looking forward to seeing you tomorrow" + intake form link if not yet completed
Post-visit check-in 24–36 hours after Email How are you feeling? What to notice in the next 48 hours. Easy rebooking link.

Phase 2 — Active Client Cadence

Staying present between visits

  • 1
    Monthly newsletter (email): 1 educational piece, 1 seasonal tip, 1 client story or outcome highlight. Not promotional — value-forward. Open rate target: 38%+.
  • 2
    Seasonal check-in (email, 4x/year): "It's [season] — common patterns we're seeing + how to prepare your body." Soft rebooking CTA. Converts dormant clients at ~14% with no discount required.
  • 3
    Birthday touchpoint (email, automated): Simple acknowledgment + a small gesture (complimentary add-on, not a discount). Highest open rate of any automated email in service businesses: avg 64%.

Phase 3 — Re-Engagement Trigger

Winning back dormant clients systematically

Trigger: Any client with no appointment in 75 days automatically enters a 2-touch re-engagement sequence. No manual monitoring required.

Touch 1 (Day 75): "We haven't seen you in a while — how are you feeling?" Genuine, not salesy. Includes a brief note about seasonal conditions relevant to their original presenting issue.

Touch 2 (Day 85, if no response): A single offer — not a discount, but a value add. "Come back for a session this month and we'll include a 15-minute check-in consultation at no charge."

At current dormant client volume (180 clients, 75+ days inactive), a 15% re-engagement rate = 27 sessions = $2,430 recovered from a 2-email sequence.

Phase 4 — Referral Mechanics

Building a referral engine that runs itself

  • 1
    Ask at the right moment — not at checkout, but in the Day 30 milestone email when satisfaction is highest and the outcome is fresh
  • 2
    Make it easy: "Know someone who could use this? Forward this email — their first visit is on me" (gift, not discount — protects your pricing)
  • 3
    Track it: use a simple referral code in your booking link so you know which clients are your best advocates
