Implementation

How to roll this out without losing existing members

  • 1
    Grandfather all existing monthly members at their current rate for 90 days with advance notice — this prevents churn and builds goodwill
  • 2
    Launch new pricing to all new clients immediately — no need to wait
  • 3
    Introduce annual tier with a "founding member" window: first 20 sign-ups at $169/mo locked for life — creates urgency and cash flow in month one
  • 4
    Send a transparent email to your community explaining the change — studios that communicate the "why" see 80% lower churn on price increases
