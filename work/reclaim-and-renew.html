Reclaim & Renew: a Clarity Audit and a live job app

A pre-launch business deciding how to enter its market. Every job involves decisions about what stays and what goes, and those decisions can turn into disputes.

Business

Type of work

The problem

Before launch, the owners needed to know where the opportunity was. Once jobs started, they needed a clear record of every item decision so nobody could argue about it later.

What I built

  • A Clarity Audit with market findings and 90-day actions
  • A live job collaboration app
  • Every item sorted into Remove, Keep, or Review
  • Photo uploads and comment threads on each item
  • Operator logins and revenue stats
