K5 Dog Services: a client portal for a dog breeder and trainer

Katie runs a breeding program, training, and boarding. She wanted out of admin so she could focus on the dogs. So I built her a portal she opens every day.

Client
K5 Dog Services
Business
Dog breeding, training, and boarding
Type of work
Client systems and automation

The problem

Every part of the business had its own moving pieces. New client inquiries. Matching families with puppies. Litters, due dates, pickups, boarding drop-offs. Paperwork for every family. It all needed tracking, and it all landed on Katie.

What I built

  • A daily home screen: intake queue, puppies available, pickups, and active clients at a glance
  • Four intake forms that feed one queue
  • A matching workbench for pairing families with puppies
  • A shared calendar for heat cycles, due dates, litters, pickups, adoptions, and boarding
  • Records for dogs, litters, puppies, and whelping cases
  • Document scanning and email alerts
  • An 11-page Welcome Packet generator
  • A website rebuild spec and an Instagram keyword automation plan
