Nurture Sync: keeping doulas with the families who need them

The two founders of Nested & Nurtured support families through one of the biggest moments of their lives. That's the work. Everything else should make room for it.

Client
Nested & Nurtured
Business
Doula practice
Type of work
Client systems and automation

What was slowing it down

Births don't keep a schedule. Matching each family with an available doula, tracking who's on call, and watching capacity across the team was a complex puzzle worked out by hand. At the same time, the founders were launching a luxury retreat. The juggling was taking attention that belonged to families.

What I built

  • Nurture Sync, a custom app for the practice
  • An intake queue for new families
  • Doula profiles and assignments
  • Team capacity: on-call births and scheduled hours for each doula
  • Approvals and Gantt-style scheduling
  • Prompts when a client has only booked one service
  • Competitive analyses, strategy documents, and a retreat partners sheet

What it makes possible

The founders see who's on call, who has room, and which new families are waiting, all in one view. Less time solving the scheduling puzzle. More presence with the families in their care.
