A purpose-built brand, live website, free family assessment tool, and operations platform for a practice dedicated to closing the family experience gap in memory care communities.

The Problem

The care was excellent. The gap was in how it felt.

Memory care communities routinely deliver skilled, compassionate care — and still lose families to early move-outs, negative reviews, and eroded trust. The problem isn't the care quality. It's the experience gap: what staff know and do versus what families see, hear, and believe.

Poor communication drives more move-outs than poor care. Staff burn out and leave because hard conversations go unsupported. Referral pipelines stall because families don't have language to recommend a community they only half-understood. The gap needed a name, a framework, and a brand that could carry it to the operators who needed it most.

The insight that shaped everything

"The quality of care and the experience of care are not the same thing. Closing that gap is where trust lives."

Before & After

From concept to credible, live presence.

Memory Care Project was built from scratch — no existing brand, no website, no framework. What existed was deep domain expertise, lived family experience, and a clear point of view on what memory care communities were getting wrong. The work was to make all of that visible and usable.

The Framework

Five pillars. One named methodology.

A core part of the brand work was making the methodology concrete and ownable. The 5 Steps Method gives operators a shared language for the work — and gives families a way to evaluate whether a community is taking experience seriously.

Pillar What It Addresses Why It Matters
01 · Communication Proactive touchpoints that keep families informed; structured support for difficult conversations The #1 driver of early move-out is families feeling left out — not care quality
02 · Trust Transparency, follow-through, and treating families as partners rather than problems Trust is built in small moments — and destroyed in single ones
03 · Education Dementia literacy for families; skills and resilience training for direct care staff Informed families escalate less. Trained staff stay longer.
04 · Engagement Meaningful connection between families, residents, and care staff across the full journey Families who feel present become a community's most credible referral source
05 · Responsiveness Systems ensuring no concern goes unanswered and no staff member feels abandoned under pressure Speed and consistency of response is what families remember most

How We Got There

Strategy first. Story second. Build third.

1
Audience & Positioning Clarity

Mapped the two audiences — families in crisis and operators seeking change — and developed distinct messaging for each. Defined "the family experience gap" as the ownable concept the entire brand would anchor to.

2
Framework Development

Structured the 5 Steps Method from existing expertise — naming and sequencing the pillars so they were teachable, referenceable, and differentiated from generic memory care consulting language.

3
Site Architecture & Copy

Built the site structure around how each audience makes decisions, not around a service list. Every section has a single job and a single reader in mind.

4
Assessment Tool & Platform Build

Built the free family assessment as a live tool — not a PDF download. Began development of the staff observation and family communication platform as a natural extension of the methodology into community operations.
